Privacy

Ethereum URPD · last updated 3 October 2026

The site

Ethereum URPD is a static page. It has no accounts, sets no cookies, runs no analytics and shows no ads, and it collects no personal data. Everything it loads, the chart's data, the charting library (Plotly.js) and the font, comes from the site itself; it connects to nothing else.

Y-axes you pin with PIN Y-AXIS are kept in your own browser (its local storage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own logs of requests under GitHub's privacy statement.

The videos on YouTube

The four full-history videos are posted to YouTube by the site's owner. The site itself only links to them: it does not embed them, and it sends nothing to YouTube. Watching a video on YouTube is covered by YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
